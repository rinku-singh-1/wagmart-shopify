# WAGMART Brand Guide

## Brand idea

WAGMART is a cheerful, dependable place to find useful everyday products for dogs and cats. The storefront should feel warm and welcoming, while product information stays clear and grounded in the real catalog.

## Logo

- Use the supplied WAGMART wordmark without recoloring, stretching, or placing it on a busy background.
- Leave clear space around the mark equal to the height of the W in the wordmark.
- Use the purple and pink mark on light cream or white surfaces. Keep a simple light background behind it so the colors stay clear.
- Suggested desktop display width: 180-210 px. Suggested mobile width: 135-165 px.

## Color palette

The primary hues are sampled from the supplied logo. Supporting colors keep the storefront readable and calm.

| Role | Color | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| WAGMART purple | `#7B46E4` | Primary buttons, links, selected states, and key accents |
| WAGMART pink | `#E23B92` | Secondary accents, badges, and small decorative details |
| Ink | `#282331` | Main headings and body text |
| Muted ink | `#6D6876` | Supporting copy and metadata |
| Cream | `#FFF9F3` | Main warm surface and hero copy panel |
| Pale lavender | `#F2ECFF` | Calm alternate surface and soft icon circles |
| Pale blush | `#FCEAF3` | Small highlight surfaces |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Product cards and high contrast space |

Use purple on light backgrounds for primary actions. Reserve pink for emphasis so it remains distinctive. Keep body text dark and avoid long text over photography.

## Typography

- Display headings: **Fredoka**, weight 500-600. Its rounded forms echo the supplied wordmark without making paragraphs hard to read.
- Body and controls: **Nunito Sans**, weights 400-700.
- Use short sentence-case headings, generous line-height, and a clear size difference between headings and body copy.
- For environments where the web fonts do not load, use rounded sans-serif fallbacks followed by system sans-serif.

## Photography and illustration

- Use the supplied catalog imagery for any product card or product-specific image. Do not invent additional products, package designs, brand marks, or product benefits.
- Use AI-generated lifestyle photography for pets, people, and settings around the products. Keep animals natural, believable, and central to the scene.
- Prefer soft daylight, warm cream interiors, subtle lavender or blush accents, and clean negative space for page copy.
- Use small, simple line icons and occasional paw or sparkle motifs. Keep decorative illustration secondary to the pet and the product.
- Do not place generated text or fake logos inside images.

## Interface style

- Overall feel: clean, warm, playful, and polished.
- Layout: generous whitespace, a centered content width, clear section rhythm, and simple grids that collapse cleanly on mobile.
- Cards: white or very light tinted surfaces, subtle borders, rounded corners around 16-24 px, and restrained shadows.
- Buttons: rounded pill or softly rounded rectangle, purple fill with white label for the primary action; white or pale lavender with purple outline for secondary actions.
- Motion: short, gentle fades and slides. Respect reduced-motion settings and provide controls for carousels.
- Icons: consistent stroke weight, simple shapes, accessible labels for icon-only controls.

## Voice

- Friendly, concise, and practical.
- Speak to pet families in everyday language.
- Avoid medical, nutrition, safety, quality, shipping, return, or sustainability claims unless WAGMART can substantiate them.
- Never create fictional reviews, customer counts, partner brands, discounts, or service promises.

## Homepage scope

The first implementation is the homepage only. The current first section is a five-slide, responsive hero. Subsequent sections should use configurable Shopify collections or selected products so the merchant can map them to products actually present in the supplied WAGMART catalog.
